Frugal Innovation Philosophy

Large-Scale Impact Through Low-Cost, Internet-Free Technology.

Rural classrooms in India face real constraints: sporadic electricity, lack of high-speed internet, and multi-grade teaching environments. Our innovations are built specifically for the realities of rural government schools.

Zero Internet Dependency

Plug-and-play offline hardware works seamlessly even in the most remote tribal villages.

Toy-Based Pedagogy

Concrete to abstract learning with physical board games, counting rods, and flashcards.

Teacher-Centric Design

Empowers existing government teachers rather than replacing them with machines.

Frugal Scalability

Designed to be 10x more affordable than conventional digital classroom setups.

Core Interventions

Our Flagship Programs

Designed to bring joyful, intuitive, and measurable learning to government primary school classrooms across India.

Digital Classroom Program

Transforms standard classroom televisions into interactive teaching hubs without requiring internet connectivity. With 800+ animated video lessons, voice-assisted rhymes, and gamified quizzes in regional languages, it ensures every child experiences world-class digital learning even in the most remote villages.

English Program

Our English Program strengthens language learning in primary education using the LSRW approach. With bilingual lessons, interactive songs, engaging worksheets, and gamified assessments, it turns English into a joyful subject. By equipping teachers with simple, ready-to-use tools, the program helps children in Classes 1–5 to build a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Math Program

Our Math Program makes mathematics fun and easy to understand in primary classes. Using hands-on teaching kits, board games, activities, and interactive worksheets, it helps children in Classes 1–5 build strong numeracy skills step by step. With structured lesson plans and joyful classroom experiences, the program empowers teachers to explain concepts clearly and ensures every child develops confidence in solving math problems.

Science Program

Our Science Program ignites natural curiosity and scientific temperament through experiential learning. With hands-on experiments, interactive 3D models, inquiry-based question sets, and real-world observations, it empowers young learners to explore foundational physics, ecology, and life science concepts with enthusiasm and deep understanding.
